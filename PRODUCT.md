EternalNotes is an Obsidian-like study workspace that keeps your knowledge private while offering grounded, citation-forward RAG tooling. The product should feel like a premium research instrument: calm surfaces, crisp hierarchy, strong states, and predictable navigation. Motion is purposeful feedback, not decoration.
